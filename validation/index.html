01 — start here

Validation is a gradient, not a badge.

The six models support different kinds of checks. The verification gradient groups them into three classes; choose a row, then open the model's validation page for the full evidence.

In plain English: Anchored to the OBR's published forecast path, the emulator tracks GDP within 0.15% and consumption within 0.25%, and stays within 0.37% on GDP run as a genuine forecast with held add-factors. In the UK structural VAR's (SVAR's) frozen-edge test, all 14 of 14 outturns fell inside its 68% uncertainty bands — a single-origin result, suggestive rather than established coverage (see the model's page for the caveat). frb-us reproduces the Fed's own database to machine precision.

Imposed or targeted matches are anchors, not validation. Stronger evidence comes from out-of-sample forecasts, independent reform costings, and solver residuals.

02 — model by model

The strongest claim, the weakest point, the full evidence.

One card per model: the strongest validated claim with its headline number, the weakest point stated as plainly, and the page where the charts, tables, and caveats live.

obr-macro — OBR emulator
✓Strongest: anchored GDP within 0.15% MAPE of the March 2026 EFO; the independent 1pp basic-rate costing lands −6.4% from HMRC's ready reckoner.
!Weakest: free-running GDP misses by 5.75%, and the spending multiplier is ~1.0 by construction against the OBR's published 0.6.
→obr-macro validation →
boe-svar — Bank of England SVAR
✓Strongest: FEVD shares of 42.1% / 49.5% against the paper's ~40% / ~50%; Bank Rate beats a drifting random walk (0.79 at one quarter, significant).
!Weakest: the CPI forecasting win does not survive a drift benchmark, and the 49-origin bands under-cover (68% band contains 57–71%).
→boe-svar validation →
frb-us — Fed macro model
✓Strongest: reproduces LONGBASE to 5.6×10−17 — machine precision; shock responses sit at the Fed's own inter-release noise floor.
!Weakest: no predictive validation is possible (LONGBASE is not a forecast), and model-consistent expectations are not implemented.
→frb-us validation →
us-hank — two-asset HANK
✓Strongest: an 18-test suite gates the Auclert et al. (2021) steady state, market clearing, and published shock responses; wealth targets hit to ~10−13.
!Weakest: replication fidelity only — it does not forecast the US economy and maps no detailed tax reforms.
→us-hank validation →
pe-microsim — tax-benefit rules
✓Strongest: household calculations are deterministic applications of implemented legislation — checkable by hand, rule by rule.
!Weakest: population aggregates inherit survey error — the 1pp basic-rate costing runs 6.4% below HMRC in year one.
→pe-microsim validation →
psl-og — overlapping generations
✓Strongest: calibration targets sit on official UK aggregates (debt-to-GDP within 0.1pp of the latest ONS print) with every imposed match labelled as imposed.
!Weakest: no published replication ground truth exists; targets met by construction are anchors, not validation.
→psl-og validation →
