PolicyEngine microsimulation

Computes what a reform costs and who gains or loses, for one household or the whole population.

  • UK · US
  • Exact for rules
  • Hosted
  • Annual
Overview Methodology Validation Use it
Exact for rules; costings static, no interval. Evidence → Household arithmetic is checked rule by rule. Population estimates add survey error.

Good for

  • Taxes, benefits and net income for one household
  • Static costings and decile incidence, UK and US
  • The static costing the OBR emulator and OG-UK start from

Not for

  • Behavioural or macro feedback: OBR emulator, OG-UK
  • Rules not yet implemented in the model (coverage)
  • Intervals on population estimates: none computed (limits)

What it returns

1p on the UK basic rate, costed on the enhanced FRS.

The same call also returns incidence by decile. Macro feedback on this yield comes from the OBR emulator.

A benchmark, not a validation. HMRC’s figure is post-behavioural and ours is static, so the gap is not an error measure (why).
