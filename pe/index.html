PolicyEngine microsimulation

Computes what a reform costs and who gains or loses, for one household or the whole population.

Overview Methodology Validation Code Paper

Exact for rules Exact for implemented rules, checked rule by rule; population estimates add survey error, and costings are static with no interval. How far to trust it →

Good for

  • Taxes, benefits and net income for one household
  • Static costings and decile incidence, UK and US
  • The costing every macro model starts from

Not for

  • Behavioural or macro feedback: OBR emulator, OG-UK
  • Rules not yet implemented in the model
  • Intervals on population estimates: none computed (limits)

What it returns

1p on the UK basic rate, costed on the enhanced FRS.

The direct yield of 1p on the basic rate, with decile-by-decile incidence, before the macro feedback the OBR emulator adds; how it compares with HMRC is on Validation. The full chain: score a reform.

With the macro models

Every bridge in the suite starts or ends here.

Reform → PolicyEngine microsimulationstatic costing → OBR emulator · OG-UKmacro feedback → Dynamic score

score_reform accepts microsim, obr, og and og+microsim. The BoE SVAR, FRB/US, US HANK and DEFINE-UK connect the other way, as incidence on households (every join in the suite).

Costings are static: behaviour and macro feedback are not included, and population figures carry survey and calibration error with no interval.
