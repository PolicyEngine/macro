01 — what it is

Statute as code, at household resolution.

The package encodes UK and US tax-and-benefit rules as parameterised statute and computes every variable for a household you describe — UK: income tax, National Insurance, Universal Credit, Child Benefit, Pension Credit; US: federal and state income tax, payroll tax, SNAP, TANF, EITC, CTC, SSI and Social Security. A reform is a flat dict of parameter path → new value, identical at household and population level.

Four layers: policyengine-core (the simulation framework, forked from OpenFisca), policyengine-uk and policyengine-us (the statutory rules per country), and policyengine — the analyst-facing package covered here, adding dataset management, reforms, structured outputs and version-pinned release bundles for reproducibility.

It feeds two macro engines: the OLG model consumes its effective and marginal rates to build tax functions; the OBR emulator takes its static costing as the direct effect and returns the second-round one. See also the code, the methodology, and how far to trust it.
