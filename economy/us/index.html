Why the US charts are shorter

The topic pages carry the current reading and, where one exists, the conditioning baseline beside it. Two of the three charts are shorter than their UK counterparts: GDPC1 is quarterly, so twenty points span 2021Q4–2026Q3, but CPIAUCSL and UNRATE are monthly, so the same twenty-point window covers only 2025-01–2026-09 and 2025-02–2026-10. And no US series in this store starts before 2020-01, where the ONS series behind the UK charts reach back to 1955Q1: the long view here is as long as the store, not as long as the published record.

Where the numbers come from

Dated, immutable snapshots; never a live call.

  • Every US series sits in the shared data store, with its snapshot history.
  • FRED returns no release dates to this fetcher, so there is no US release calendar.
  • None of these series is scored: no model here makes a US forecast.
