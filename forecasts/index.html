05 — what this is not

Limits worth stating plainly.

→One scored round proves little. Nothing here demonstrates forecasting skill until the scored count is well into double figures — at least a year away.
→The bands run too narrow in hindsight testing. Across 49 historical origins the 68% band contains outturns about 62% of the time and the 90% band about 77% — both under-cover, worsening with horizon. Full study on the validation page.
→CPI does not beat a fair naive benchmark. Against a random walk with drift the CPI advantage largely evaporates (1.03 at eight quarters); Bank Rate is the only robust win in that exercise, and it is not yet a forecast variable here.
→Three variables, no fixed calendar. UK GDP growth and CPI (boe-svar) plus unemployment (Okun satellite); rounds so far follow data-edge refreshes rather than a schedule, so round spacing is itself a discretionary choice. Each round records its estimation sample; coefficients are not re-estimated every round.
