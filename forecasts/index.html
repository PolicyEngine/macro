05 — what this is not

Limits worth stating plainly.

→One scored round proves little. No skill claim until the scored count is well into double figures — at least a year away.
→The bands run too narrow. In hindsight testing the 68% band covers ~62% and the 90% band ~77%, worsening with horizon; the fans above are re-calibrated for this. Full study on the validation page.
→CPI does not beat a fair naive benchmark. Against a random walk with drift the advantage largely evaporates; Bank Rate is the only robust win, and it is not yet forecast here.
→Three variables, no fixed calendar. GDP growth and CPI (boe-svar) plus unemployment (its satellite); rounds follow data-edge refreshes, not a schedule, and coefficients are not re-estimated every round.
