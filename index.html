How the models connect

Every link runs through the microsimulation. Hover or tap a model.

  • Out: reform cost macro feedback
  • Back: long-run wages dynamic score
  • In: macro path who gains and loses
MicrosimulationUK + US · the core

  • Its CPI path drives benefit-uprating incidence.

  • Its wage and hours paths drive employment-income incidence.

  • Its wage and labour responses drive employment-income incidence.

  • Its scenario income deltas drive employment-income incidence.

  • A reform's static cost goes in as an add-factor; GDP feedback comes out. UK only.

  • Long-run wage and rate changes feed a second microsimulation run. None published.
