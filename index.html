02 — validation

What the evidence proves—and what it does not.

Each model is checked against the strongest available benchmark. Replication, forecast performance, statutory correctness, and calibration are different kinds of evidence—and where no independent check exists, we say so. Compare the evidence →

obr-macroAnchored replication

Reproduces the March 2026 EFO path to 0.15% GDP MAPE under published anchors; free-running results are weaker.

boe-svarForecast evaluation

0.32pp RMSE across 14 evaluated outturns; all fell inside the 68% ranges.

frb-usShock replication

Shock responses match current pyfrbus 1.1.1 to 1.4×10⁻⁸.

us-hankPublished replication

Replicates Auclert et al. (Econometrica 2021); outputs remain stylized and first order.

pe-microsimStatutory checks

Household rules are checked against statute; population estimates inherit survey uncertainty.

psl-ogCalibration only

Calibration targets are met by construction; no independent outcome benchmark exists.
