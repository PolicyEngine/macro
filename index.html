Where each model fails

One verdict per model, in words, linked to the tests behind it.

  • PolicyEngine microsimulation pe-microsim legislation Household arithmetic is exact, to the penny; population runs add survey error, and the HMRC costing comparison is a benchmark, not a validation. Evidence for the PolicyEngine microsimulation →
  • OBR emulator obr-macro published anchor Tracks the March 2026 EFO by construction; weak free-running. The household tax route behaves; the spending multiplier is fixed by construction. Evidence for the OBR emulator →
  • BoE SVAR boe-svar published anchor Replicates the paper’s GDP decomposition, short on CPI; no forecast skill against a drifting random walk once the tests are adjusted together. Evidence for the BoE SVAR →
  • FRB/US frb-us published anchor Matches the Fed’s own pyfrbus inside its two releases’ disagreement; no predictive claim. Evidence for FRB/US →
  • US HANK us-hank published anchor Solves Auclert et al. (2021); the headline targets are inputs, not results. Evidence for US HANK →
  • OG-UK psl-og no ground truth Targets met by construction; no independent outcome benchmark exists. Evidence for OG-UK →
  • DEFINE-UK define-uk partial replication Baseline replicates the manual; scenarios are design-gated and paper-anchored. Deltas only, never levels. Evidence for DEFINE-UK →

The forecast record

Every round is archived first and scored later; the misses stay on the record.

  1. Archive the roundCommitted to Git before the outturn exists
  2. Wait for the outturnScored when the ONS publishes the period
  3. Score it three waysAgainst the outturn, a naive baseline and the OBR
  4. Keep the data as publishedDated snapshots, never edited, so a score can be reproduced

How the models connect

Every join starts or ends at the microsimulation; no macro model feeds another.

  1. A reform’s cost goes out to the OBR emulator

    Reform→Microsimulationcost · who pays→OBR emulatormacro feedback→Scored result

  2. Long-run wages come back from OG-UK

    Reform→OG-UKlong-run wages→Microsimulationsecond run→No scored result

  3. A macro path comes in as incidence

    BoE SVAR, FRB/US, US HANK or DEFINE-UKeconomy-wide path→Microsimulationwho pays→Who gains and loses
