Evidence → A variance-share comparison with the Bank of England’s paper: global shocks’ share of GDP variance consistent with it, of CPI variance 8.5pp short; no forecast skill against a drifting random walk once the tests are adjusted together.

Good for

  • What is hitting the economy now, as six named shocks
  • GDP and CPI forecast paths with raw, uncalibrated 68% and 90% bands
  • Why the forecast changed since last quarter

Not for

  • Scoring a statutory reform: score_reform does not accept it (limits)
  • Policy counterfactuals: OBR emulator, OG-UK
  • Beating a simple benchmark: no forecast skill (the test)
  • A return-to-target inflation path: no inflation anchor, so medium-term CPI stays near 3.1%, the recent trend (limits)
