BoE SVAR

Decomposes UK GDP and inflation into six named shocks, and forecasts both with bands.

Overview Methodology Validation Code Paper

Partial replication GDP decomposition consistent with the Bank of England’s paper, CPI short of it; no forecast skill against a drifting random walk once the tests are adjusted together. How far to trust it →

Good for

  • What is hitting the economy now, as six named shocks
  • GDP and CPI forecast paths with raw, uncalibrated 68% and 90% bands
  • Why the forecast changed since last quarter

Not for

  • Scoring a statutory reform: score_reform does not accept it (limits)
  • Policy counterfactuals: OBR emulator, OG-UK
  • Beating a simple benchmark: no forecast skill (validation)

svar_inflation_incidence prices the gap between the median CPI path and the March 2026 EFO through the next April’s uprating of Universal Credit and Child Benefit, decile by decile (every join in the suite).

An experimental illustration, not a scored reform. It uses the forecast median and a curated slice of CPI-linked benefits; the state pension and frozen thresholds are excluded.
