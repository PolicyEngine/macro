An overlapping-generations model for PolicyEngine reform scoring

Download PDF All papers

What the paper establishes: OG-UK is calibrated; it has no outcome benchmark.

More detail

Almost every quantity in its calibration table is an imposed input that cannot disagree with the official aggregate; the one substantive gap is the labour share; and the genuinely over-identifying checks, the capital–output ratio and the steady-state interest rate, have no published reconciliation yet.
