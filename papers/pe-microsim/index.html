Exact rules, estimated aggregates: the PolicyEngine tax–benefit microsimulation in an open model suite

Download PDF All papers

Working paperNo DOIVahid Ahmadi · PolicyEngine

Household arithmetic is exact and carries no uncertainty. A population estimate is a weighted sum of that arithmetic over an estimated population. The two must not be blurred.

The costing benchmark against HMRC and every limit are on the validation tab.
