Exact rules, estimated aggregates: the PolicyEngine tax–benefit microsimulation in an open model suite

Download PDF All papers

Working paperNo DOIVahid Ahmadi · PolicyEngine

The paper’s claim in one line: household arithmetic is exact and carries no uncertainty; a population estimate is a weighted sum of that arithmetic over an estimated population, and the two must not be blurred. The costing benchmark against HMRC and every limit are on the validation tab.
