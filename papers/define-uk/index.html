DEFINE-UK: running and gating an unlicensed ecological stock-flow consistent model

Download PDF All papers

What the paper establishes: one gate passes, one diverges, two close at a ceiling.

More detail

The manual’s Table 4 macro block replicates, with 2025 growth clearing a tolerance set after the run; baseline emissions diverge and are pinned rather than tolerated; and with no numeric v1.1 scenario results published, the scenario targets are closed at the achievable ceiling, not passed. The upstream is unlicensed and the baseline sits far from outturns, so the adapter serves deltas, never levels. The numbers are on Validation →
