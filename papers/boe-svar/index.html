An open replication of the Bank of England structural VAR

Download PDF All papers

Working paperVahid Ahmadi · PolicyEngine

An open replication that matches the Bank’s GDP variance decomposition, falls short on CPI, and shows no forecast skill over a random walk with drift once the tests are adjusted together.

Data vintagenotes

Current forecast: data through 2026Q1, outlook 2026Q2–2029Q2. Every figure is on the validation tab. The replication and evaluation figures come from the model repository’s 2026Q2 data refresh (boe-var-model #24); the values before it are at 845e2a4.
