obr-macro · UK · hosted · 42 pages

An open OBR macro model for UK fiscal scoring

Download PDF All papers

Working paperNo DOIVahid Ahmadi · PolicyEngine

The paper’s claim in one line: the OBR’s published equations, transpiled and anchored, reproduce the EFO by construction and score the household tax route with a behavioural second round; the direct levers are reported as an identity, a dead channel and a slow-converging closure.

Every figure is on the validation tab.
