An open OBR macro model for UK fiscal scoring

Download PDF All papers

Free-running against the March 2026 EFO baseline, real GDP is 4.48% MAPE and consumption 7.49%, with company profits 63.29% off and 6 of 11 computed variables in band. The anchored fit — GDP 0.15%, consumption 0.25% — is one test, not two: every other expenditure component is exogenous or pinned, so the anchored GDP error is the consumption error to the pound. And one of the four fiscal levers contains behaviour: government consumption is a pure accounting identity (impact multiplier 1.0000 at any shock size, against the OBR's 0.6 decaying to zero), government investment is dead with a wrong-signed GDP residue, corporation tax never converges, and only the household-tax route responds.
