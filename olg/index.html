OG-UK overlapping generations

OG-UK

Shows where the UK economy settles under a tax reform, and the year-by-year path there.

  • UK
  • Calibrated
  • Runs locally
  • Long-run steady state
Overview Methodology Validation Use it
Targets met by construction; no outcome benchmark. Evidence → ONS and OBR aggregates are calibration targets. The nearest external benchmark, OBR Working Paper 22 Table 5.1, has not yet been run.

Good for

  • Long-run effects of a tax reform on GDP, work, saving and capital
  • Year-by-year transition paths, and eight industry sectors
  • Wages back to the microsimulation for a dynamic score

Not for

  • Forecasts (why)
  • Quarterly dynamics: OBR emulator
  • Who gains and loses this year: PolicyEngine microsimulation

What it returns

A stylised reform, not announced policy, against baseline on the committed transition path.
