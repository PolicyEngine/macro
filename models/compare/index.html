Models are in the hub order, not ranked. The sourced benchmark sits in the first row; each model row gives its peak effects and a verdict, and Details holds the run, full units and caveats.

How to read the verdicts

Each verdict starts with one of six words: plausible, off, questionable, unresolved, not tested or no instrument; some add a qualifier (such as “off, sign right”), and each carries its reason and the open issue. These words grade one experiment; each model’s overall standing uses the status vocabulary on its model card. Results file version 2026-11-06.3; how it is checked and regenerated.

What does a 100bp rise in the policy rate do?

A 1pp rise in the policy rate. The models do not share a rate path, so each row states its own and the size of the path in pp-quarters (annualised rate × quarters).

Four of five models answer. Off: OBR emulator and BoE SVAR; plausible: FRB/US; plausible in-model: US HANK. DEFINE-UK has no instrument for it.

DEFINE-UK UKNo instrumentDEFINE-UK has no monetary-policy instrument.

GDP per pp-quarter divides the peak GDP effect by the rate path's size to the peak. HANK's figure uses the realised real-rate path; the other rows use the nominal policy rate, which is close to the real rate in FRB/US because its inflation barely moves. On HANK's rstar path (10 pp-quarters at ρ = 0.9) the figure would be about 0.04%, so HANK's 0.16 and FRB/US's 0.15 are not like-for-like.

HANK reports inflation as a quarterly rate; this page multiplies it by 4 to give an annual rate.

Is a 100bp cut the mirror image of a 100bp rise?

Four of five models answer. Off: OBR emulator; not tested (linear): BoE SVAR and US HANK; plausible: FRB/US. DEFINE-UK has no instrument for it.

What does government spending of 1% of GDP do to output?

Higher government purchases worth about 1% of GDP. The multiplier is the GDP change divided by the spending change, both in % of GDP. Financing and the monetary rule differ by model and drive most of the spread.

Four of five models answer. Off: OBR emulator; plausible: FRB/US; questionable: US HANK; unresolved: DEFINE-UK. BoE SVAR has no instrument for it.
